Verso — lyrics & metadata for your music. Finds missing lyrics (plain + synced LRC) via 5 free sources (LRCLIB, AMLL, KuGou, Lyricify, LunaBeat), fixes incomplete tags (MusicBrainz + iTunes, review before writing), fetches album art, embeds or saves .lrc files, and scrobbles to ListenBrainz / Last.fm. Offline-first, no tracking, no Play Services.
