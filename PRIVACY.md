# Privacy Policy — LyricTag

LyricTag is offline-first and collects nothing.

- **On-device:** your library scan, lyric cache, settings and review decisions
  stay in the app's private storage. No accounts, no analytics, no ads,
  no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind.
- **Network (only when you tap fetch/search):** the app sends the track's
  title, artist, album and duration to lyric services to look up matching
  lyrics. Enabled sources (all free, keyless, no accounts):
  - **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net)
  - **AMLL** (api.amll.dev)
  - **KuGou** (mobiles.kugou.com / lyrics.kugou.com)
  - **Lyricify** (api.lyricify.app, ISRC via api.credits.fm)
  - **LunaBeat** (static TTML index on GitHub Pages)
  No device identifiers, no location, no file contents. Sources are tried in
  order and can be toggled individually in Settings.
- **Metadata lookup (only when you tap Fetch metadata):** title/artist/album
  are sent to **MusicBrainz** (musicbrainz.org) to find matching release data.
- **Scrobbling (only when you enable it):** played track name, artist, album
  and play time are sent to **ListenBrainz** and/or **Last.fm** using tokens
  and API keys **you** paste in Settings. Your Last.fm password is used once
  to obtain a session key and never stored. Queued plays are kept on-device
  until submitted.
- **Poweramp integration:** when Poweramp asks for lyrics, the app answers
  with lyrics text for that track. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- You can clear the lyric cache any time in Settings → Privacy.
